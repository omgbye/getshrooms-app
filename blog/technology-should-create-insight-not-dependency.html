Technology Should Create Insight, Not Dependency

By Angel Reyes · November 17, 2026

I find it strange that so much self-improvement software measures its success by how frequently someone returns.

If an application genuinely helps you improve your life, shouldn't you eventually need it less?

My ambition is to design around something harder to quantify than daily engagement: the occasional epiphany. That moment when a question, a connection, or an unexpected possibility fundamentally changes how you understand yourself.

There's something fascinating about combining centuries-old philosophical ideas with modern computational systems. Not because software possesses wisdom, but because thoughtful engineering and design can make meaningful information easier to explore.

SHROOMS is still evolving. Some ideas will work, others won't, and the product will undoubtedly change through testing.

But the objective remains simple: I don't want to build another application that occupies space on your phone while accomplishing absolutely nothing.

I want to build something that leaves you with more than you arrived with.

And if it helps even one person discover a possibility they hadn't previously considered, that's a better outcome than another meaningless streak.
