Turning a Mushroom Into Software

By Angel Reyes · November 17, 2026

The name was deliberately unconventional. But what began as a whimsical concept evolved into something more interesting from an engineering perspective.

A mushroom is the visible expression of a much larger biological system. Beneath it exists mycelium, a network of interconnected filaments. I became fascinated with translating that structure into software.

What if someone's interests, abilities, ambitions, experiences, and contradictions could be explored as interconnected information rather than reduced to a generic personality label?

That idea became the foundation of SHROOMS: structured questions, career domains, visual relationships, and recommendations designed to encourage exploration rather than dictate someone's future.

I'm not inventing the wheel. Personality assessments, career guidance, and self-reflection have existed for generations. The challenge is assembling familiar concepts into a digital experience that feels genuinely useful.
