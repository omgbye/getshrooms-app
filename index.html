ABOUT SHROOMS

A little clarity before you begin.

What is SHROOMS?

SHROOMS combines an AI guide with guided self-discovery and practical planning. It helps you explore your purpose, possible career paths, and what you want to make room for in your life.

How does SHROOMS help me find a direction?

THE TRIP asks about your interests, values, experiences, and circumstances. THE REVEAL presents three directions to explore. Choose one and THE PATH helps you turn it into steps that fit the time you have.

What does the AI coach do?

The Shaman is your conversational AI guide. You can talk through what interests you, ask about your next step, and reflect on what you learned by trying it.

Can I change direction?

Yes. You can explore alternatives and use what you learn from small experiments to reconsider your next move. Your results describe possibilities to explore.

What does “Technology as Spirituality” mean here?

It is the philosophy behind SHROOMS: using technology to pay attention to your experience, question inherited expectations, and explore what feels meaningful through real-world action.
